Chapter 04 · The people who matter

My Family

A collection of beautiful moments with my family.

Family is where many of my favourite memories begin.

My family is an important part of my life. They are my source of love, support and strength. The moments we share are memories I will always treasure.

I am grateful for the people who have been part of my journey.

A letter from my heart

For my late Grandpa

There is one person in my family whose memory I carry with me every day — my late grandpa.

I still remember how he waited for me to come back. I am truly grateful that I managed to return and had the chance to take care of him at the hospital. I will always be thankful that I could be there by his side before he passed away.

It has been a year, but so many little things still remind me of him. Sometimes, a photograph, a familiar place or an old conversation brings those memories back. I miss him, and I wish I could have more time with him. Even so, I will continue to keep his love and the moments we shared close to my heart.

Some people may no longer be beside us, but the love they leave behind never really leaves.
